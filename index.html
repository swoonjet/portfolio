UNMAS × Critical Mass

Sweeper

Cannes Lions · New York Festivals · Communication Arts

A virtual minefield at the New Museum in New York. The Sweeper app used iBeacon technology to trigger audio explosions on visitors’ phones as they crossed the exhibit, followed by stories of the damage landmines cause and a call to donate to UNMAS. Landmines kill or maim about ten people a day in more than 60 countries; this made it personal.

House of Anansi × Critical Mass

Massey Lectures App

Cannes Lion, Design

An iPad platform for more than 50 years of the Massey Lectures: nearly 200 hours of material, each e-book paired with its five-hour audio lecture series, contextual content and author interviews, plus in-line questions and comments that turned reading into a conversation.

City of Toronto · Centre for Independent Living

The Inaccessible Transit Shelter

Communication Arts · Canadian Marketing

A transit shelter sealed entirely in glass so nobody could get inside. The headline: “Feeling left out? Over 400,000 Torontonians with disabilities do, too.” A call to action led to a site with ways to make Toronto more accessible.
